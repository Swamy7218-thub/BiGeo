FAQ

Common Questions

Google and HERE Maps work great for urban structured addresses. They fail completely on rural unstructured addresses like "near banyan tree, Yellareddyguda" — which is how 800M rural Indians actually describe their location. BiGeo uses Claude AI + a 1M+ rural location knowledge base assembled from PMGSY, LGD and India Post data. We resolve what Google Maps returns "no result" for.

92% of rural Indian addresses resolve with a confidence score above 0.75. Each response includes a confidence_score (0–1) and a gps_source field so you know exactly how reliable the result is. For the remaining 8%, we return the best available pincode centroid with a lower confidence score — never a silent failure.

Customers or agents send a WhatsApp message to your BiGeo business number in any Indian language. Our IndicBERT NLP model extracts pickup address, delivery address, package description and COD amount from natural conversation. No form, no app, no English required. The booking is created instantly and the customer gets a tracking link.

Yes. BiGeo is a certified logistics service provider on the ONDC network, implementing Beckn protocol v1.2. Orders from any ONDC buyer app automatically flow into your BiGeo dashboard. We push real-time status updates back to the ONDC network and handle the address enrichment before dispatch.

All data is stored in AWS ap-south-1 (Mumbai) — never leaves India. We comply with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023: consent management, right-to-erasure API, and 90-day log retention. Customer PII is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3.
