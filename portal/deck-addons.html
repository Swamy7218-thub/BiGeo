Customer Personas

Three buyers.
Same pain. One API.

Every customer loses money because of unresolvable rural addresses. BiGeo fixes it at the API layer for all of them.

🚚
E-Commerce & Logistics
The Logistics Operator
Delhivery, Shadowfax, Porter, Meesho sellers, ONDC merchants doing rural COD delivery at scale.
Their Pain

27% RTO rate on rural orders. ₹180 wasted per failed delivery. Call centres overwhelmed with "cannot find address" tickets.

BiGeo Win

Pre-validate every address before dispatch. RTO drops from 27% to under 8%. ₹180 × 10,000 deliveries = ₹18L saved monthly.

Delhivery Shadowfax Porter Ecom Express ONDC LSPs
🏦
Rural Fintech & Banking
The Rural Lender
Aye Finance, Spice Money, Avail Finance, MFIs doing KYC, loan disbursement and insurance in Tier 3/4 markets.
Their Pain

KYC address verification fails for 40%+ of rural applicants. Field agents spend 3 hours locating first-time borrowers. Fraud risk from unverifiable addresses.

BiGeo Win

Verify any rural address to GPS in <2s during KYC. Field agent gets exact coordinates before the visit. Fraud risk drops with GPS-stamped address verification.

Government & Public Services
The eGovernance Vendor

BiGeo Win

Bulk geocode beneficiary lists at ₹2–5/1000 calls. GPS-stamp every PM-KISAN, PM Awas or ration card record. Audit trail with confidence scores.

The mandate: ONDC's Phase 3 rural expansion requires all logistics service providers (LSPs) on the network to handle Tier 3/4 addresses. This makes rural address resolution a compliance requirement, not a nice-to-have. Every LSP on ONDC needs BiGeo or something like it — and nothing like it exists.
How BiGeo fits into ONDC
1
Buyer places order on ONDC app
Types address in local language — "నీటి ట్యాంక్ దగ్గర, భూంపల్లి, సిద్దిపేట"
2
BiGeo API resolves address
IndicBERT NER → Fable 5 → HERE Maps → returns lat: 18.103, lng: 78.847, confidence: 0.92
3
LSP accepts & routes the order
Carrier gets verified GPS, OSRM optimises route, rider navigates precisely — not by guesswork
4
Delivered. Status pushed to ONDC.
RTO drops from 27% → 8%. Merchant, buyer and ONDC network all get real-time status via Beckn protocol
