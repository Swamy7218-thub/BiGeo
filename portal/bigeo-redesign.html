The Problem

Rural logistics is systemically broken.

Despite producing the majority of India's goods, rural producers capture less than 40% of final value — due to failures no single actor has solved.

🔗
4–5×
Layers of Middlemen
Producers lose up to 60% of potential margin passing through exploitative intermediary chains before goods reach urban buyers.
🛣️
73%
Villages Without Reliable Transport
Broken first-mile connectivity forces producers to sell locally at distressed prices rather than accessing competitive urban markets.
📦
30–40%
Post-Harvest Waste
Without local grading and packaging, entire batches are rejected on arrival at urban warehouses — a ₹92,000 crore annual loss for India.

0
Logistics Tech Built for Rural-to-Urban
Every logistics platform — from Dunzo to Delhivery — is architected for urban-to-urban or urban-to-rural. Nobody has solved the other direction.
The BiGeo Model

The missing middle, built.

A tech-enabled physical network bridging rural producers and urban demand. Not an app — an operating system for rural commerce.

🔗
Unified Platform
A single interface connecting producers, local transport, QA hubs, and long-haul logistics. One login. One dashboard. Every stakeholder.
🚜
First-Mile Optimization
Aggregating small loads from multiple producers at village level to make pickups economically viable — the step every platform has skipped.
🏭
Quality Assurance Hubs
Modular, tech-enabled local hubs for grading, sorting, and packaging before long-haul. Eliminates urban rejection at source.
🧠
AI Route Optimization
Dynamic routing algorithms built for rural constraints — unpaved roads, seasonal disruptions, irregular vehicle availability.
↩️
Reverse Logistics
Structured returns and rejection handling so a failed urban delivery doesn't wipe out a producer's monthly income.
📡
End-to-End Transparency
Dynamic package IDs providing real-time tracking and provenance data from village to buyer doorstep. Built for ONDC.
