ABC ROAD CARRIERS
Freight Rate Agreement - FY 2025-26
Effective: 01-Apr-2025 to 31-Mar-2026

Party B (Transporter): ABC Road Carriers, Hyderabad
Party A (Shipper): Sample Pharma Pvt Ltd

Rates agreed as below (all rates inclusive of loading, exclusive of GST):

Hyderabad to Vijayawada - 20ft container / 9MT - Rs 18000 per trip
Hyderabad to Vijayawada - 32ft SXL / 16MT - Rs 24500 per trip
Hyderabad to Bengaluru - 32ft SXL / 16MT - Rs 32000 per trip
Hyderabad to Chennai - 32ft SXL / 16MT - Rs 38000 per trip
Hyderabad to Nagpur - 20ft container / 9MT - Rs 21000 per trip

Detention: First 1 day free at loading point and 1 day free at unloading point
for all lanes above. Beyond free days, detention chargeable at Rs 1500 per day
per vehicle, subject to proof of arrival/departure timestamp at both ends
(gate-in / gate-out register or POD remark).

Diesel escalation: Base diesel price considered Rs 92/litre (Hyderabad).
For every Rs 5/litre increase over base, add 2% to all per-trip rates above.
Escalation applied quarterly based on average IOCL retail price.

Loading/unloading charges included in above rates. Toll extra as per actuals
against receipts.

This agreement supersedes all prior verbal rate understandings between the
parties for the above lanes.
